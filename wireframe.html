Data model notes

What the app tracks. Wireframe stage only, nothing is stored yet.

Player
name, position, team, tier
Contributor
name, login, role (you or friend)
Ranking
contributor, settings profile, ordered list of players, status (draft or published)
Consensus ("Ball Knowers")
derived from all published rankings, per settings profile
League
platform, scoring, team count, superflex, dynasty or redraft, budget
Roster
league, team, players on it
Trade
assets given, assets received, ranker used, result
Lineup
roster, week, starters, bench, suggestion notes

Screens

Home, Rankings, Trade Calculator, Start/Sit, Contributor, plus a Sync League modal. Bottom nav switches the three core tabs.
