Project brief

Your Fantasy Football Rankings Site

The one thing

A fantasy football rankings platform with a beautiful UI that presents your personal rankings and analysis.

Problem

You want your own voice and opinions out there in the fantasy football space, presented exactly the way you want it to look, not constrained by existing tools.

Users

Two groups: you (building your rankings, your analysis, your platform) and other fantasy players who come to use your rankings, trade calculator, and start/sit advice.

Solution

A rankings site where you publish your player rankings, let friends add theirs, and provide tools (trade calculator that handles any league type, start/sit calculator, league sync that applies your rankings to users' actual teams). The UI is the hook: beautiful and attractive, the kind of site people want to stay on.

User journey

  1. 1User lands on the home page
  2. 2Sees their synced league (or a prompt to sync their league if they haven't yet)
  3. 3Bottom navigation bar gives access to the core features: Rankings, Sit/Start, Trade Calculator
  4. 4User taps into Rankings to see your player rankings (and can add their friends' rankings)
  5. 5User runs a trade through the Trade Calculator (works for any league type variation)
  6. 6User checks Start/Sit to get your take on their lineup decisions
  7. 7User syncs their league so your rankings apply directly to their teams
